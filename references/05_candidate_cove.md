# 05 — Candidate Generation and CoVE
STRICT: max 3 viable candidates.
EXPLORE: 3–10 hypotheses only if evidence supports them.

Never create candidates incompatible with CRITICAL Must-haves or filler candidates. Text-derived candidates remain hypotheses until independently corroborated. Stop alternatives after STRONG.

For each survivor run 3–6 CoVE checks from distinct evidence channels:
text/identifier; façade; object geometry; street/layout; neighbouring structures; skyline; terrain; vegetation; road/sign standard; coastline; map geometry; geotagged imagery; reverse image.

Each check: `PASS | FAIL | ND | NOT_EXECUTED | SKIPPED`.

Define 1–2 candidate-specific killer checks.
`KILLER FAIL → REJECT`
`CRITICAL FAIL → REJECT`

A killer confirms only when its positive candidate-specific condition is actually observed.

Before final identification, ask: what single available observation would falsify this candidate fastest? Execute it if decisive and available.


## Candidate competition

Before STRONG:
- identify the nearest serious competitor when one exists;
- run at least one differential check;
- verify that the proposed killer check is `UNIQUE` or `DISCRIMINATIVE`;
- do not treat multiple compatible clues as equivalent to competitor falsification.


## Visible shortlist

Maintain a shortlist of surviving candidates throughout verification.

At output time, expose up to the top 3 survivors. Preserve lower-ranked survivors until they are explicitly rejected or blocked by a hard gate.

Do not collapse the shortlist to only the leader before final reporting.

## Minimum hypothesis generation

Unless a unique/direct identifier creates a legitimate exact lock, generate at least 3 distinct non-filler hypotheses before aggressive elimination.

The 3 hypotheses should differ meaningfully in geography or identity and must arise from current-image evidence, search-derived evidence, or valid current-image user bounds.

After falsification, keep the best 3 in the candidate ledger for final reporting, including those that become WEAK or REJECTED.
